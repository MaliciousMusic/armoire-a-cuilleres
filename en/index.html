01L’Armoire

A cosy little chocolate nook in a lane of old Clermont

A stone’s throw from the cathedral, on rue des Chaussetiers, L’Armoire à Cuillères is a tiny hot chocolate bar and tea room: mismatched vintage cups, mint-green tables, books on top of the old cupboard and a golden glow that makes you want to stay.

Twelve single-origin hot chocolates, from ivory white to 99 % dark, made to order with organic milk from a farm in Auvergne. To go with them, cakes baked in-house every day: fondants, cheesecakes, tarts and cookies.

4.6/5 on Google, over 600 reviews

04FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Where can I find the best hot chocolate in Clermont-Ferrand?

At L’Armoire à Cuillères, 11 rue des Chaussetiers, 63000 Clermont-Ferrand, in old Clermont: a hot chocolate bar offering twelve single-origin hot chocolates (from 33 % white to 99 % dark), made to order with organic Auvergne milk and served at around 65 °C.

Do I need to book for afternoon tea?

No: for afternoon tea, just drop in. The tea room is small; on weekdays and in the early afternoon it’s easier to find a seat.

How do I book Sunday brunch?

By phone on 04 63 22 63 56. Brunch is served on Sundays, €23.00 per person with an all-you-can-eat buffet; seats are limited.

Can I order a whole cake?

Yes, for individuals and businesses alike, at least 48 hours in advance, using the order form on our website. You can then follow your request live.

Do you have plant-based milks and lactose-free options?

Yes: oat, coconut or almond with every drink. For allergies, ask us about the ingredients in our cakes.

How does the loyalty card work?

It’s free and lives on your phone: we scan your QR code at every visit, and on your 10th stamp, you get a free hot drink and pastry.

Does L’Armoire à Cuillères serve coffee and tea?

Yes: espresso, cappuccino, caffè latte, mocha, chai latte, matcha latte and around ten teas and herbal infusions, hot or iced.

Are the cakes homemade?

Yes, every day, in our little kitchen on rue des Chaussetiers: chocolate fondants, cheesecakes, brownies, tarts, loaf cakes and cookies. The cakes of the day are on the chalkboard.
